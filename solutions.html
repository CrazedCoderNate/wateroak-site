Solutions

Technology that earns its keep

Every system we deliver is measured against the same standard: it should save you money, save you time, or open up revenue you could not reach before. Here is how we do it across four practice areas.

App Creation

Application Development

Off the shelf software forces your team to work the way the software works. We flip that. Our applications are built around your actual process, whether that is a customer portal, a field service app, or an internal tool that ties three departments together.

Everything ships production ready: tested under load, secured from day one, and backed by version controlled code your team can inspect at any time. We build with proven technologies like React, Node.js, Python, and Java, chosen for longevity rather than novelty.

  • Web and mobile applications that work on every device your team and customers use
  • Internal tools and dashboards that give managers live answers instead of week old spreadsheets
  • System integrations that connect the software you already own so data stops living in silos
  • Automated testing and deployment pipelines so updates ship in minutes, not weekends
  • Full documentation and handover so you are never dependent on a single vendor
AI Integration

AI That Does Real Work

AI is only valuable when its output can be trusted. We have shipped production AI systems where automated workers are paired with automated reviewers that check every result before it reaches a customer or a database. That validation first approach is standard in everything we build.

We work directly with the leading AI platforms, including the Claude and OpenAI APIs, and we tune models for your workload so you get accurate results at a fraction of the usual running cost. Where privacy matters, we can run models on your own hardware.

  • AI assistants that answer customer and employee questions from your own documents and data
  • Document processing that reads invoices, contracts, and forms in seconds instead of hours
  • Workflow automation with built in quality gates, so nothing unchecked reaches production
  • Custom model tuning that cuts AI running costs while improving accuracy on your tasks
  • Monitoring and guardrails so you always know what your AI is doing and what it costs
Cloud Service Development

Cloud Systems Built to Scale and Built to Last

Your infrastructure should grow when business grows and cost less when things are quiet. We design cloud systems on AWS and modern container platforms like Kubernetes that do exactly that, with the databases, security, and monitoring handled from the start.

This is the discipline we practice daily: keeping payment systems and live services online for thousands of simultaneous users. Uptime, backups, and disaster recovery are not add ons in our work. They are the foundation.

  • Cloud architecture on AWS using serverless and container platforms that scale automatically
  • Database design and management across MongoDB, Elasticsearch, DynamoDB, and RDS
  • Migration from aging servers to modern cloud infrastructure with zero data loss
  • Monitoring and alerting that catches problems before your customers do
  • Disaster recovery planning with tested backups and clear runbooks for the worst day
In-House Tech Solutions

Own Your Software, Stop Renting It

Most companies pay for large software suites and use a fraction of the features. The per seat fees climb every year, the data lives on someone else's servers, and switching away feels impossible. That is by design.

We build focused replacements for the tools you actually use: scheduling, customer tracking, inventory, quoting, reporting. You pay once to build it, own it outright, and shape it to your workflow instead of paying forever for features you never touch. For many clients the build pays for itself in one to two years of canceled subscriptions.

  • Software spend audit that maps what you pay for against what your team actually uses
  • Focused replacements for CRM, scheduling, inventory, quoting, and reporting tools
  • Clean data migration out of your current vendors and into systems you control
  • No per seat pricing: add as many users as you want without the bill going up
  • Your data on your terms, stored where you choose and exportable at any time
